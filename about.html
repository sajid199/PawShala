Meet Our Founder

Atish - Founder & Pet Care Specialist

With over 5 years of experience in pet care and animal welfare, I founded PuppyAdda with a vision to create a bridge between loving families and adorable puppies who need forever homes.

"Every puppy deserves a loving family, and every family deserves the unconditional love that only a furry friend can provide. At PuppyAdda, we don't just facilitate adoptions - we create lifelong bonds and bring joy to homes across the country."

My journey in pet care began with my own rescue dogs, and I've been passionate about animal welfare ever since. I personally ensure that every puppy in our care receives the best possible treatment, proper vaccinations, and lots of love before they find their forever homes.

When I'm not busy matching puppies with families, you'll find me training with our furry friends, researching the latest in pet nutrition and care, or volunteering at local animal shelters.

Our Story

At PuppyAdda, our journey began with a simple love for dogs and a dream to make every puppy find a forever home. Founded in 2020, we've been dedicated to connecting adorable puppies with caring families across the country.

Our team is passionate about animal welfare, ensuring every puppy is healthy, vaccinated, and ready to bring joy to their new homes. We work closely with trusted breeders and shelters to provide a seamless adoption process.

Our Mission

Our mission is to promote responsible pet adoption and care. We believe every puppy deserves a loving family, and every family deserves the joy of a furry companion. Beyond adoption, we offer grooming, training, and pet care services to support pet parents throughout their journey.

We strive to educate our community about responsible pet ownership, ensuring every doggo dost lives a happy, healthy life.
