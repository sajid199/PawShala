About PuppyAdda

Connecting loving families with adorable puppies since 2020

Get in Touch

Our Story

At PuppyAdda, our journey began with a simple love for dogs and a dream to make every puppy find a forever home. Founded in 2020, we’ve been dedicated to connecting adorable puppies with caring families across the country.

Our team is passionate about animal welfare, ensuring every puppy is healthy, vaccinated, and ready to bring joy to their new homes. We work closely with trusted breeders and shelters to provide a seamless adoption process.

Contact Us

Our Mission

Our mission is to promote responsible pet adoption and care. We believe every puppy deserves a loving family, and every family deserves the joy of a furry companion. Beyond adoption, we offer grooming, training, and pet care services to support pet parents throughout their journey.

We strive to educate our community about responsible pet ownership, ensuring every doggo dost lives a happy, healthy life.

Why Choose Us

Healthy Puppies

All our puppies are vaccinated, vet-checked, and raised with love to ensure they’re ready for their forever homes.

Trusted Breeders

We partner with ethical breeders and shelters to ensure the highest standards of care and transparency.
